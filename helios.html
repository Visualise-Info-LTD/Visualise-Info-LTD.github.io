The information exists. The context is scattered.

Enterprise information is often divided across specialist systems, documents and physical locations. Understanding an asset, issue or activity can mean manually reconstructing the picture before work can begin.

The digital representation

The environment is the foundation

Helios creates a faithful, navigable representation of the physical environment. It gives teams a shared visual reference for understanding where things are, how spaces relate and what surrounds an asset or issue.

Point clouds, panoramic imagery, maps and spatial points of interest make that environment intuitive to explore. The operational value comes from the information and workflows connected to it.
