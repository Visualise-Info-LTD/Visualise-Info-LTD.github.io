Please read our privacy policy carefully to get a clear understanding of how we may collect, use, protect or otherwise handle your PII in accordance with our website.

01

Cookies and website usage technologies

Optional analytics require your choice.
We use optional analytics and website usage technologies to understand how visitors use the website and to improve it. These technologies are not loaded unless you select "Accept analytics". If accepted, they may use first-party cookies and browser local or session storage. Usage information may include page URLs and navigation paths, pointer movement, clicks, scrolling, visual representations of how pages are displayed and used, browser, device and viewport details, and IP address.

Remembering your choice.
We use browser local storage to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics. This preference is used to apply your choice and does not itself activate analytics. You can review or change your choice using the "Privacy choices" control available on each page.

Contact form technologies.
Third-party form-delivery and anti-spam technologies are loaded on the page containing the enquiry form to operate and protect that form. When you submit an enquiry, these services process the information you provide. They may use cookies or similar technologies required to provide and protect the form.

02

Collection of Personal Information

We may collect personal data that you provide when you submit the website enquiry form, contact us directly, or engage in transactions or other activities where personal information is required for processing. If you accept optional analytics, website usage and technical information may also be processed for the analytics purposes described above.

03

Use of Your Personal Information

The personal information we collect is used solely for the purpose it was provided and related purposes as permitted by law, such as:
